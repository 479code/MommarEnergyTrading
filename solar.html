The equipment

How a solar system works.

Solar panels

Panels capture energy from the sun. The number of panels installed depends on the energy demand of the individual or organisation.

Inverter

The inverter stores the energy in the battery for later use, and switches to battery power automatically once the sun goes down.

Batteries

Batteries keep power available after dark, and can also be charged from a generator or mains supply.

Why solar, why now

A greener energy mix for Nigeria.

Solar is one of the fastest-growing energy sources in the world, used by homes and businesses for electricity, heating and cooling, and by utilities at scale to power cities and towns.

Nigeria’s plans to go green are helping businesses and industries use solar to diversify their energy sources, improve efficiency and save resources. MOMAR has established solar energy solutions for both individuals and organisations.
