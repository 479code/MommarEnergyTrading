03 / Haulage & logistics

Overview

Moving petroleum products safely and on time takes planning at every step. MOMAR coordinates tanker haulage and bulk fuel deliveries for our clients, from loading at the depot to offloading at the receiving site.

We plan delivery schedules around each client’s consumption and storage capacity, manage dispatch, and coordinate the trucks and drivers assigned to each movement.

Every movement is documented and reconciled, so the client can match what was loaded against what was received. That record matters most for aviation fuel and for institutional customers who need a clear audit trail.

Our haulage service supports our petroleum products supply, including Jet A-1 for aviation customers.
