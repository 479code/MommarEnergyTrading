The project

A strategic fuel-storage project.

MOMAR has entered into a 15-year lease agreement to construct a state-of-the-art aviation fuel depot for storing, blending and selling petroleum products.

The depot will be positioned for strategic fuel storage, third-party storage and supply to the market.

  • Strategic fuel storage
  • Third-party storage
  • Blending
  • Supply to the market

Planned market development

Two horizons.

Aviation fuel for a strategic client

In the short term, the tank farm will be positioned as an aviation Jet A1 fuel depot, supplying primarily to a strategic client.

JET A1

Wider markets and airlines

The long-term goal is to supply petroleum products to various markets and commercial airlines.
