CNG and alternative energy.

Development of compressed natural gas (CNG) facilities and the equipment, storage and logistics behind them, alongside alternative-energy projects.

06 / CNG & alternative energy

Overview

Compressed natural gas is a cleaner, lower-cost alternative to petrol and diesel for vehicles and industry, and Nigeria is investing in the infrastructure to make it widely available.

MOMAR supports CNG projects from facility development to distribution. We support mother and daughter station projects, procure CNG storage and refuelling equipment, and coordinate the logistics that move gas between stations.

Alongside CNG, we coordinate alternative-energy infrastructure projects and source renewable-energy equipment where our operational capability supports it.

CNG installations are regulated facilities. We undertake them only with the applicable permits and approvals in place, and with competent technical personnel.
