06 / CNG & alternative energy

Supporting Nigeria’s move to gas.

We support CNG mother and daughter station projects: facility development, storage and refuelling equipment, and the logistics that move gas between stations.

We also coordinate alternative-energy infrastructure projects and source renewable-energy equipment where our operational capability supports it.

Scope

What we provide.

Contact us for a quotation on any of the following.

  • CNG facility development and associated infrastructure
  • CNG storage and refuelling equipment procurement
  • CNG distribution logistics
  • Support for CNG mother and daughter station projects
  • Alternative energy infrastructure project coordination
  • Renewable energy equipment sourcing

CNG installations are regulated facilities. Projects proceed with the applicable permits, approvals and competent technical personnel in place.
