Our commitments

Four responsibilities.

Our success depends on the success of the people and places around our work.

01

Our people

We adhere to the highest safety standards for our staff in whatever location or project they are engaged in, and constantly review our HSE policies to keep them in line with globally acceptable standards.

02

Host communities

We support host communities by investing in projects that benefit them. We employ, equip and train people from those communities, because we know our success depends on theirs.

03

Government & local content

We support the Government’s Local Content Policy and adhere to the country’s constitution.

04

The environment

We work to reduce our carbon footprint by avoiding activities that create negative environmental impacts, and by investing in renewable energy.
