04 / Installation & maintenance

Overview

MOMAR builds, installs and rehabilitates fuel storage and industrial facilities. Our scope covers the civil works, storage tank installation and rehabilitation, pipework, and the mechanical equipment that moves and measures product: pumps, filtration and metering.

We have delivered this work for the Nigerian Army and the Nigerian Air Force. At the Nigerian Army Aviation Depot in Minna we built a complete Jet A-1 facility, from groundworks to the finished product bay. At NAF Base Kaduna we rehabilitated and upgraded an eight-tank bulk fuel installation.

We also offer equipment inspection, preventive maintenance, materials supply and facility upgrades for existing installations.
