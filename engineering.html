02 / Infrastructure, engineering & equipment

Overview

MOMAR builds, installs and rehabilitates the infrastructure that stores and moves petroleum products: tank farms, storage tanks, loading gantries and fuel distribution systems, together with the civil, mechanical and piping works around them.

We also supply and install the equipment these facilities run on, including pumps, compressors, tanks, valves and instrumentation, and maintain it after handover.

We are currently building a Jet A-1 aviation fuel tank farm at Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport, Abuja, and have delivered this work for the Nigerian Army and the Nigerian Air Force. At the Nigerian Army Aviation Depot in Minna we built a complete 100,000-litre Jet A-1 facility, from groundworks to the finished product bay. At NAF Base Kaduna we rehabilitated and upgraded an eight-tank bulk fuel installation.
