Cookie policy.

This website does not use cookies.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device, often to remember you or to measure how the website is used. Similar technologies include local storage, tracking pixels and third-party scripts.

What this website uses

None. This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising, social media or other tracking technologies. It does not load content from other websites: our fonts, scripts and images are all served from our own website.

Because we use no cookies, we do not show a cookie banner or ask for cookie consent.

Forms

When you send an enquiry or a supplier registration, the details you type are sent to our website host’s form service so that they reach our team. This does not use cookies. How we handle those details is explained in our privacy notice.

Hosting

Like any website, our host (Netlify) processes technical information such as your IP address to deliver pages and keep the website secure. This does not involve cookies on your device.

If this changes

If we ever add cookies or analytics that are not strictly necessary, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting them.
