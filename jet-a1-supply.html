Project overview

About this project

ClientNigerian Air Force
ProductJet A-1 aviation fuel
BasesKaduna, Katsina and Bauchi
ScopeAviation fuel supply

MOMAR Energy Trading Company Ltd. supplies Jet A-1 aviation fuel to Nigerian Air Force (NAF) bases in Kaduna, Katsina and Bauchi States.

Jet A-1 is the kerosene-type fuel used by turbine-engined aircraft. Air operations depend on it being available at each base, on time and to specification.

At NAF Base Kaduna, MOMAR’s work also includes the rehabilitation and upgrade of the base’s bulk fuel installation: eight Jet A-1 storage tanks with a total capacity of 464,000 litres.

The contract

Aviation fuel for three air bases.

The three Nigerian Air Force bases MOMAR supplies with Jet A-1.

  • NAF Base Kaduna Kaduna State
  • NAF Base Katsina Katsina State
  • NAF Base Bauchi Bauchi State
