The project

A new aviation fuel facility, built from the ground up.

MOMAR constructed a Jet A-1 aviation fuel storage facility for the Nigerian Army Aviation Depot at Bola Ahmed Tinubu International Airport, Minna.

The works covered the site and civil works, the gatehouse and perimeter, horizontal Jet A-1 storage tanks, and a covered product bay with pumps, filtration, metering, valves and pipework.

  • Civil works and site preparation
  • Jet A-1 storage tanks
  • Pump, filtration and metering bay
  • Valves, pipework and fittings
  • Gatehouse and perimeter
