Army Aviation Jet A‑1 facility.

A 100,000-litre Jet A-1 aviation fuel storage facility for the Nigerian Army Aviation Depot at Bola Ahmed Tinubu International Airport, Minna, Niger State.

100,000 LJet A-1 storage capacity
7 monthsSeptember 2024 to April 2025
Jet A‑1Aviation turbine fuel
Army AviationNigerian Army Aviation Depot, Minna

Project overview

Dedicated aviation fuel storage, built from the ground up.

The brief

The Nigerian Army Aviation Depot at Minna needed its own storage for Jet A-1, so military aviation could rely on fuel being handled safely and available on site.

What we did

  • Civil works, site preparation and concrete hardstanding
  • Horizontal Jet A-1 tanks on concrete plinths, with ladders and handrails
  • A covered product bay: pumps, filter vessels, flow meters, valves and pipework
  • Perimeter wall, steel gates, gatehouse and solar street lighting

The result

Handed over in April 2025: 100,000 litres of dedicated Jet A-1 storage, supporting the safe and reliable handling of aviation fuel for military aviation operations.

From site to handover

How it was built.

Seven months from the first groundworks to a working facility.

  3. Fit-out

    Valves, flow meters and fittings connected and finished.

  4. April 2025

    Handover

    The completed facility handed over to the depot.
