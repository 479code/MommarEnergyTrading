ScopeConstruction of an aviation fuel storage facility

MOMAR Energy Trading Company Ltd. constructed a Jet A-1 aviation fuel storage facility for the Nigerian Army Aviation Depot at Bola Ahmed Tinubu International Airport, Minna, Niger State.

The facility was built from the ground up. The site works included concrete hardstanding at the entrance and around the facility, and concrete plinths carrying the horizontal steel storage tanks, which are fitted with access ladders and handrails.

At the centre of the facility is a covered product bay. It houses the product pumps, filter vessels, flow meters, gate valves and the pipework and fittings that connect them.

The site is enclosed by a perimeter wall with steel gates and a gatehouse at the entrance, and is lit by solar-powered street lights.
