Project overview

About this project

ClientNigerian Air Force
LocationNAF Base, Kaduna
Capacity8 tanks × 58,000 L = 464,000 L
ScopeRehabilitation and upgrade

MOMAR Energy Trading Company Ltd. rehabilitated and upgraded the bulk fuel installation at the Nigerian Air Force (NAF) Base, Kaduna, turning it into a modernised aviation fuel storage facility.

The installation comprises eight Jet A-1 aviation fuel storage tanks of 58,000 litres each, giving a total storage capacity of 464,000 litres.

The depot is connected to the tarmac, so bowsers have easy access to refuel aircraft directly from the facility.
