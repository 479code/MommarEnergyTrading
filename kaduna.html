NAF Base Kaduna fuel installation.

Rehabilitation and upgrade of the bulk fuel installation at the Nigerian Air Force (NAF) Base, Kaduna, into a modern Jet A‑1 storage facility.

Project overview

A modernised aviation fuel depot, still in our care.

The brief

The bulk fuel installation at NAF Base Kaduna needed rehabilitating and upgrading into a modern storage facility for aviation fuel.

What we did

  • Rehabilitated and upgraded the existing bulk fuel installation
  • Eight Jet A-1 storage tanks of 58,000 litres each
  • A depot connected to the tarmac, so bowsers can refuel aircraft directly
  • Ongoing management of the facility on behalf of the Nigerian Air Force

The result

A modernised depot holding 464,000 litres of Jet A-1, completed in 2015 and managed by MOMAR on behalf of the Nigerian Air Force ever since.
