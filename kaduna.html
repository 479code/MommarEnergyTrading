The project

Eight tanks. 464,000 litres of Jet A-1.

MOMAR rehabilitated and upgraded the bulk fuel installation at NAF Base Kaduna, which comprises eight Jet A-1 aviation fuel storage tanks of 58,000 litres each, a total storage capacity of 464,000 litres.
