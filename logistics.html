03 / Logistics, quality & project delivery

Overview

Moving petroleum products and delivering energy projects both depend on coordination. MOMAR plans and manages product transportation from loading to delivery, and brings the same discipline to the projects we build: engineering, procurement, construction supervision and handover.

Quality runs through both. Fuel is inspected and checked at every stage, and works on site are supervised for quality, compliance and progress.

This coordination supports our petroleum products supply, including Jet A-1 delivered with our own tanker fleet to Nigerian Air Force bases, and the infrastructure projects we deliver for government clients.
