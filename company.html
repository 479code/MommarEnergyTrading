About MOMAR Energy Trading Company Limited

Head office · 8 Danube Close, Maitama, Abuja

MOMAR Energy Trading Company Limited (M.E.T) is an indigenous Nigerian energy company operating across the Oil & Gas and Renewable Energy industries, providing integrated energy solutions, technical services and infrastructure support to clients across the public and private sectors.

Our operations span the petroleum, natural gas and renewable energy value chains, with capabilities encompassing Petroleum Products Supply; Infrastructure, Engineering & Equipment; Logistics, Quality & Project Delivery; Natural Gas Solutions; and Renewable Energy Solutions.

Through our petroleum products supply division, we facilitate the sourcing, procurement, marketing and supply of refined petroleum products, including Premium Motor Spirit (PMS), Automotive Gas Oil (AGO), Dual Purpose Kerosene (DPK), Aviation Turbine Kerosene (ATK/Jet A-1) and other specialised petroleum products. We also provide infrastructure, engineering and equipment solutions, covering the development, installation, maintenance and rehabilitation of oil and gas facilities, storage systems, mechanical equipment and associated infrastructure.

Our logistics, quality and project delivery operations support efficient product transportation, distribution, technical inspection, quality assurance, quality control, health, safety and environmental (HSE) management, as well as comprehensive project coordination and execution. In the natural gas sector, we provide solutions across Compressed Natural Gas (CNG), Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG), Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) and pipeline natural gas, including supply, distribution, transportation and gas infrastructure development.

Complementing our conventional energy operations, MOMAR Energy is committed to advancing sustainable energy development through renewable energy solutions, including solar photovoltaic systems, hybrid and off-grid power installations, battery energy storage systems, wind energy, hydropower, biomass and other emerging renewable energy technologies.

By integrating international industry standards, technical expertise and best practices in quality assurance, quality control, health, safety and environmental management, we strive to exceed clients’ specifications, maintain operational excellence and deliver projects safely, efficiently, on schedule and within budget.

At MOMAR Energy, we are committed to building lasting business relationships, delivering dependable energy solutions and contributing to Nigeria’s energy security, industrial growth and sustainable development.
