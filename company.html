About MOMAR Energy

Delivering Integrated Energy Solutions

MOMAR Energy Trading Company Limited (M.E.T) is a Nigerian-headquartered integrated energy, engineering and infrastructure company with over 15 years of combined operational experience, delivering solutions across petroleum products supply, natural gas, engineering, construction and renewable energy.

Mission & Vision

Guided by our core practices

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, innovative and integrated energy, engineering and infrastructure solutions through technical excellence, operational efficiency and a commitment to quality, safety and sustainable development.

Our Vision

To become a leading integrated energy and infrastructure solutions company, recognised for operational excellence, industry innovation and sustainable value creation across global markets.

Core Practices

  • IntegrityUpholding transparency, accountability and ethical business conduct.
  • ExcellenceMaintaining high standards in service delivery and operations.
  • SafetyPrioritising people, assets and environmental protection.
  • InnovationEmbracing technology and improved methods to deliver better solutions.
  • ReliabilityBuilding trust through consistency and dependable performance.
  • SustainabilitySupporting responsible energy development and long-term value creation.

History

Our ambition to drive progress and deliver integrated solutions continues to shape our journey.

Headquartered in Nigeria, MOMAR Energy has grown its capabilities across the energy, engineering and infrastructure sectors, driven by technical excellence, strategic partnerships and a commitment to sustainable growth.
