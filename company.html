Company profile

Rooted in Nigeria. Built to deliver.

Head office · 8 Danube Close, Maitama, Abuja

MOMAR Energy Trading Company Ltd. is a Nigerian oil, gas and energy services company headquartered in Abuja.

We supply petroleum products, including aviation fuel, and we build, rehabilitate and maintain the facilities that store and dispense them. Our work includes a Jet A-1 storage facility for the Nigerian Army Aviation Depot in Minna, the upgraded bulk fuel installation at NAF Base Kaduna, and Jet A-1 supply to Nigerian Air Force bases in Kaduna, Katsina and Bauchi.

Alongside fuel and infrastructure, we provide petroleum haulage and logistics, procurement of technical equipment and supplies, and CNG and solar energy solutions.
