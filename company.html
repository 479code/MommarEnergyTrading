About MOMAR Energy Trading Company Limited

Head office · 8 Danube Close, Maitama, Abuja

MOMAR Energy Trading Company Limited (M.E.T) is an integrated energy, engineering, construction and infrastructure solutions company headquartered in Nigeria, with a broad scope of capabilities spanning the global energy and industrial value chains. We provide comprehensive solutions, technical services, equipment, infrastructure development and project delivery support to government institutions, international organisations, energy companies, industrial operators, corporate enterprises and private-sector clients.

Our business operations are structured around five core divisions: Petroleum Products Supply; Infrastructure, Engineering & Equipment; Logistics, Quality & Project Delivery; Natural Gas Solutions; and Renewable Energy Solutions. Through these divisions, we deliver integrated services that address the evolving requirements of the energy, construction, industrial, commercial and infrastructure sectors.

Our petroleum operations encompass the sourcing, procurement, marketing, supply and distribution of refined petroleum products, including Premium Motor Spirit (PMS), Automotive Gas Oil (AGO), Dual Purpose Kerosene (DPK), Aviation Turbine Kerosene (ATK/Jet A-1) and other specialised petroleum products. We support diverse customer requirements through structured procurement, quality assurance, transportation, storage and bulk supply arrangements.

Across infrastructure, engineering and construction, MOMAR Energy undertakes a broad range of building and civil engineering works, industrial developments, structural installations and specialised infrastructure projects. Our scope includes residential, commercial, institutional and industrial buildings; roads and drainage systems; warehouses and logistics facilities; oil and gas installations; storage terminals; mechanical and electrical systems; equipment supply; facility rehabilitation and maintenance. We also provide engineering coordination, procurement and construction support for projects across multiple sectors.

Our logistics, quality and project delivery capabilities encompass transportation, haulage, freight coordination, procurement, supply-chain management, technical inspection, quality assurance, quality control, health, safety and environmental (HSE) management, project supervision and execution support. We integrate these capabilities to facilitate efficient resource mobilisation, operational reliability and coordinated project delivery.

Within the natural gas sector, we provide solutions across Compressed Natural Gas (CNG), Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG), Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) and pipeline natural gas. Our service scope includes gas sourcing, procurement, supply, transportation, distribution, virtual pipeline solutions, gas infrastructure development and associated installation and engineering services.

Complementing our conventional energy operations, MOMAR Energy is committed to supporting the global transition towards cleaner and more sustainable energy systems. Our renewable energy portfolio encompasses solar photovoltaic systems, hybrid and off-grid power installations, battery energy storage systems (BESS), solar mini-grids, wind energy, hydropower, biomass, biogas and other emerging renewable energy technologies. We focus on delivering scalable energy solutions that promote efficiency, resilience and long-term sustainability.

Our approach is guided by internationally recognised industry practices, technical competence and a strong commitment to quality, health, safety, environmental responsibility and regulatory compliance. We strive to deliver solutions that meet applicable technical specifications, contractual requirements and project standards, while maintaining efficiency, accountability and cost effectiveness throughout the project lifecycle.

At MOMAR Energy, we believe in the power of integrated capabilities, strategic collaboration and technical excellence to deliver lasting value. We are committed to developing enduring business relationships, supporting infrastructure and industrial advancement, strengthening energy security and contributing to sustainable economic development across the markets we serve.
