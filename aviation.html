Aviation fuel, from depot to aircraft.

Jet A-1 supply and logistics, and the storage facilities, tanks and pipelines that keep aircraft fuelled.

02 / Aviation fuel & infrastructure

Our most proven capability.

MOMAR has built a Jet A-1 aviation fuel storage facility for the Nigerian Army Aviation Depot in Minna, rehabilitated and upgraded the bulk fuel installation at NAF Base Kaduna, and supplies Jet A-1 to three Nigerian Air Force bases.

We combine product supply with the infrastructure that stores and dispenses it, so one partner is accountable from the fuel itself to the facility that holds it.

Scope

What we provide.

Contact us for a quotation on any of the following.

  • Jet A-1 fuel supply and logistics
  • Aviation fuel storage facility construction
  • Bulk fuel installation rehabilitation and upgrades
  • Fuel storage tanks, pipelines and associated equipment installation
  • Aviation fuel logistics and haulage coordination
  • Installation and maintenance of air navigation and communication equipment

Air navigation and communication equipment work is undertaken subject to the appropriate technical authorisations.
