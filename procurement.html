Procurement and technical supplies.

Sourcing and supply of process equipment, instrumentation, piping materials and MRO supplies for oil, gas and industrial operations.

05 / Procurement & technical supplies

Overview

MOMAR sources and supplies the technical equipment and materials that oil, gas and industrial facilities depend on. We work from the client’s specification, identify approved manufacturers and vendors, and manage the supply through to delivery.

Our range covers rotating equipment such as pumps, compressors and gas turbine components; process equipment including reactors, columns, heaters and heat exchangers; storage tanks and pressure equipment; instrumentation and control systems; and valves, fittings and piping materials.

Because we also install this kind of equipment on our own projects, such as the pumps, filtration and metering at our Minna aviation fuel facility, we understand what it has to do once it arrives on site.

We support one-off orders, project packages and ongoing maintenance, repair and operations (MRO) supply.
